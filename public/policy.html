Collecting and Using Your Personal Information

Types of Data Collected

Personal Data

While using Our Service, We may ask You to provide Us with certain personally identifiable information that can be used to contact or identify You. Personally identifiable information may include, but is not limited to:

  • Email address
  • First name and last name
  • Account username and authentication identifiers
  • Transaction references and deposit verification records

Usage Data

Usage Data is collected automatically when using the Service. This may include information such as Your Device's Internet Protocol address (e.g. IP address), browser type, browser version, the pages of Our Service that You visit, the time and date of Your visit, the time spent on those pages, unique device identifiers and other diagnostic data.

Tracking Technologies and Cookies

We use tracking technologies (such as cookies) to track activity and to maintain reliable service performance. We use both Session and Persistent Cookies for:

  • Necessary / Essential Cookies: Essential to provide services, authenticate users, and prevent fraudulent use of user accounts.
  • Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies: Record user consent choices for seamless compliance across sessions.
  • Functionality Cookies: Remember preferences such as theme configuration, active tool sessions, and localized display parameters.

Use of Your Personal Data

The Company may use Personal Data for the following purposes:

  • To provide and maintain Our Service, including monitoring platform performance and uptime.
  • To manage Your Account: To administer Your registration and provide access to features available to registered users.
  • For the performance of a contract: Fulfillment of purchase orders, wallet funding, and service requests placed through the platform.
  • To contact You: By email, SMS, or equivalent electronic notifications regarding operational updates, order confirmations, or security alerts.
  • To manage Your requests: To attend to customer support tickets and technical inquiries.

Text Messages & Notifications Privacy Notice

If You opt in to notifications or SMS updates, We collect only the required delivery coordinates. No mobile phone numbers or consent records are shared or sold to third parties or affiliates for promotional purposes. You may opt out anytime by replying STOP or contacting support.

Retention of Your Personal Data

The Company retains Personal Data only for as long as is necessary for the purposes set out in this Privacy Policy:

  • User Accounts: Retained for the duration of the account relationship plus up to 24 months post-closure to resolve any follow-up issues.
  • Support Correspondence: Retained up to 24 months for quality assurance and dispute resolution.
  • Diagnostic Server Logs: Retained up to 24 months for security audits and threat mitigation.

Security of Your Personal Data

We implement commercial encryption standards and secure transport protocols to protect your Personal Data. Payment card details are never stored on DIH HUB servers and are processed directly through PCI-compliant automated payment gateways.

Delete Your Personal Data

You have the right to request access to, amendment of, or deletion of Your personal information. You may initiate deletion requests by contacting us directly at contact@dihhub.site.

Children's and Minors' Privacy

The Service is not directed to anyone under the age of 16. If We become aware that We have collected Personal Data from a minor under 16 without parental consent, We take immediate steps to remove that information from Our records.
